Privacy Policy

App: Note +  |  Package: com.promo.dreaming.noteplusapp  |  Last updated: May 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Note + ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy. The App is designed to store your notes, tasks, and alarms locally on your device. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties, except as described below.

1. Information We Collect

The App may access the following data only when you explicitly use the relevant feature:

Permission Purpose Storage
Camera Capture photos to attach to your notes Local only
Read Contacts Attach contact information to a note for reference Local only
Record Audio Record voice memos to attach to notes Local only
Notifications Show alarm and task reminder notifications Local only
Internet Google Drive backup/sync, Firebase services, ads External (optional)
Storage / Files Save attachments (images, documents) for notes Local only

2. Data Storage

All notes, tasks, alarms, attachments, and settings are stored exclusively on your device using an encrypted local SQLite database. We do not have access to this data.

If you use the optional Google Drive backup feature, your data is synced to your own personal Google Drive account under your control. We do not have access to your Google account or Drive files.

3. Third-Party Services

The App integrates the following third-party services that may collect data under their own policies:

  • Google AdMob — Displays advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. See: Google Privacy Policy
  • Firebase (Google) — Used for push notifications (FCM) and optional cloud features. See: Firebase Privacy
  • Google Drive API — Used only if you enable backup. Data goes to your personal Google account.

4. Permissions We Do NOT Abuse

  • Contact data is never uploaded to any server — it stays in your note locally.
  • Audio recordings are never transmitted — stored only in your note's private app storage.
  • Camera photos are never shared outside the app without your action.

5. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, please contact us.

6. Data Security

Your note data is stored in an encrypted SQLite database on your device. We implement reasonable technical measures to protect your data. However, no method of storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Delete data — Clear app data via Android Settings → Apps → Note + → Clear Data
  • Revoke permissions — Via Android Settings → Apps → Note + → Permissions
  • Delete the app — Uninstalling removes all locally stored data

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
